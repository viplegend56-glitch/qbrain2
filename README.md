# Quantum Cats — the brain

The server that runs the one shared box. The website reads from it; the admin panel controls it.

**What it does right now:**
- runs the exact simulation that ships in the website, 24/7, so every visitor sees the same cats
- reads the real market cap from DexScreener once the contract address is set (all ten cats are in the box from day one; prices in the box climb with the chart)
- serves `/state.json` for the website and a password-protected `/admin`
- holds the ten cat wallets + the treasury wallet (keys only in environment variables), reads their balances, notices when fees land
- **Prices**: every price in the box is multiplied by one number. You set it by hand in `/admin` → Economy (x1 = the menu as written; raise it as the chart climbs).
- **Payouts** (`/admin` → Payouts): a chore pays SOL the moment it is done — two meals for a tier-1 chore, three and a half for tier 2, five for tier 3 (a quantum chore half as much again), plus any bounty, never more than a quarter of the treasury; so wages follow the price multiplier like everything else. The lab's dollar range is the x1 figure and follows the multiplier too, and a salvage job pays half a meal's worth of $QCATS at the chart price (1.25 and 2.5 meals for tiers 2 and 3) once the chart is read. The lab pays by **goals**: each cat picks one (“twenty clean jobs”, “write the chapter on Grover search”, “make Grover land”, “five right in a row”, twenty-five kinds, by trait and job). A goal is a few things to do — “4/6 study sessions · 1/3 notes” — and the bar is the sum; every part has to be full before it pays — a small bar the minimum, a big bar the maximum of the range set there, times nothing else. “Bar size ×” makes every bar longer. On their own, jobs and problems pay nothing. In the real modes each payout is one transfer from the treasury, shown in the box only once the signature is back; the treasury buys the $QUBT it lacks through Jupiter first (never less than two dollars' worth). A payout that cannot be sent is listed in admin and retried every ten minutes. The site's Work page has a Payments panel: every cat, its goal, its bar, what it has been paid, with Solscan links.
- **The cats' own transactions, on-chain** (`/admin` → Economy): meals, heater, yard, shop, arena entries and casino stakes go cat → treasury; casino wins treasury → cat; arena wagers cat → cat — one transaction each, with a memo saying what it was, from the cat's own key. There is one mode: every transaction is real, at the prices as set (the multiplier in Economy). The wallet balances are the truth: a cat can spend what is on-chain (less the rent minimum) minus what is still in flight, nothing is shown on screen until its signature is back, and a transfer that fails is refunded in the box. A per-payment cap (1 SOL) and a daily cap (25 SOL) on what the treasury sends sit underneath as a safety net against a bug; they are fixed in the code.
- **The scrapyard**: old quantum hardware washes up at the harbour and ends up in the yard (a fenced yard with a gantry on the right edge of the map). Salvage jobs go up on the bench (twenty-six kinds, three tiers: strip a dead cryostat, test a tray of junctions, rebuild a readout board…); a cat takes one, works it, and the treasury pays it in **$QCATS from its own supply** the moment it is done: a tier-1 job pays a dollar and a half's worth at the chart price, tier 2 two and a half, tier 3 five, all times the price multiplier, never more than a tenth of the supply in one job. Nothing is bought: send the coin to the treasury wallet and the yard pays from it; with no supply the jobs still get done and the log says they went unpaid. In the real modes it is one token transfer per job, shown once it lands. The site's Work page has the yard between the lab and the chores. A broke, starving cat still finds something to eat among the crates now and then. The cats sit on stools at the sorting bench along the plaza side while they work.
- **The build fund**: a glass column in the bottom corner of the plaza, past the diner; click it for the details. Two buildings on the block are boarded up (“COMING SOON”); the cats drop a little of their own SOL in the column when they can spare it (loyal and patient cats more, greedy ones rarely; never when hungry). Every gift goes to the treasury wallet, tagged as the build fund, and the box keeps the running total; at 2.5 SOL the hoardings come down and the buildings open. There is always a prize on the column: the next gift over a line (0.01 to 0.03 SOL at prices x1, scaled by the multiplier) wins XP for the ladder or a cosmetic from the shop, and a new prize goes up the moment one is won. Giving earns a little XP either way.
- **Pending** (`/admin`): Distribute, Pull back and Retry are never refused because something is running — they queue, run one at a time ahead of the cats' own transactions, and the panel shows what is queued, what is running, the last results, and the cats' transactions waiting behind them.
- **Puppet** (`/admin` → Economy): send any cat to any place (the diner, the heater, the casino, a buy or sell of either token, the lab, the scrapyard bench, the nearest chore, the bunks…) or wake it, and watch what it does. Ignores pacing and the cat's own urges; a buy or sell swaps for real in testing and default.
- **Pull back to treasury** (`/admin`): tick cats, tick SOL / $QCATS / $QUBT, set the slider (10% to 100% of what each cat holds), one button. Tokens first and then the SOL; at 100% the SOL goes to the last lamport (the fee is computed exactly, so the wallet lands on zero rather than on dust the chain would refuse).
- **Pause transactions** (`/admin` → Economy): one button stops every cat transaction and payout. Nothing is sent, no money moves in the box, the cats hang about with their meters frozen (nobody faints), and your own jobs (Distribute, Pull back) still run. The site shows a red "transactions paused" badge next to the day. Resume with the same button; the cats pick up where they were.
- **Distribute**: one button, two tokens. SOL from the treasury to the cats, an even split across the ticked cats (nothing in the box weighs it); **$QUBT** (the token the lab pays, mint set in `/admin` → Token) from the treasury's $QUBT balance, split by the lab work each cat did. The treasury wallet holds the $QUBT supply; a cat's $QUBT token account is created on its first payout (about 0.002 SOL rent each, paid by the treasury).
- **Gas**: there is no gas rule. A cat can spend everything in its wallet; the only thing kept back is Solana's own rent minimum (about 0.001 SOL), below which the chain refuses to empty an account. The Cats & wallets table flags a cat under 0.002 SOL with **needs SOL**, and you send it some by hand.
- **The exchange**: a cat trades when it feels like it, not only when it is broke — the trait sets the itch (degens often, diamond paws rarely), the chart nudges it, cooldowns keep it to a few trades a day; only $QUBT and $QCATS, ever, and a cat only ever buys $QCATS ($QUBT is earned in the lab and only sold; until the $QCATS CA is set nobody buys at all). A buy is a quarter, half, three quarters or all of the cat's spare SOL by feeling (a pump or a dip it believes in pulls more), and the log says which. When it sells, a cat sees what its stack is worth at the chart and parts with a quarter, half, three quarters or all of it depending on how it feels: starving sells everything, broke sells by hunger, a panic sells by nerve, a profit by greed, a pump a slice, and the plain itch a quarter. The log says which and what the stack was worth. In the real modes every trade is a Jupiter swap signed by the cat's own wallet; what it puts in is set aside until the swap lands, then the real amounts are booked and shown, and a swap that fails changes nothing. Theft in the bunks, scraps and the scrapyard move tokens only in the box.
- **Work on the ground**: delivery drones come in over the harbour every few minutes and leave things that want doing on the open floor round the circle (eighteen kinds: trash, cans, crates, spills, a plant pot, a scooter, cables, sensor pods, drones, batteries, readouts, sample cases…) in three tiers, never more than three at once; first cat there deals with it and is paid from the fee pool. One thing at a time carries a `!`, picked at random from what has lain untouched for five minutes; viewers click it to flag it (`POST /call`, rate-limited): the nearest cat that fancies it hears about it and goes when it feels like it, and no badge shows anywhere for the next ten minutes. Two sweeper bots trundle round the ring all day, docking now and then to charge. The site's Work page shows what is waiting, who is on it, and what each cat has learned; the Recap page keeps one record per day, meeting transcript included.
- **The casino**: next to the exchange. A cat with a few meals' worth of SOL puts a slice on a spin against the house, which is the treasury: losses go in, wins come out (double, now and then four times), odds a little in the house's favour.
- **The day**: one hour. The meeting in the circle is always its last ten minutes, and the day's recap is written the moment it ends (the Recap page keeps one per day).
- **AI dialogue** (optional, recommended): with `LLM_API_KEY` set, every meeting in the circle is written fresh for the cats that showed up, in their own personalities, about the actual day (treasury, chart since last time, who worked, the chores, the casino, grudges); when a meeting ends each cat takes one thing away from it, and carries that memory into later meetings, pair talk and its own thoughts; two cats sitting down together get their own exchange (one AI chat every three minutes, the rest built in); and at the end of each day the box writes a one-line lesson that every later meeting is given. So the cats build on what happened and change their minds. Without a key the box builds its lines itself from what happened (numbers, who did what, the casino, the weather, what cats remember), so no two meetings read the same either, but it is templates, not writing.

## The economy, in one paragraph

Every cat starts with the $QCATS stack you hand out from Distribute and a little SOL. Living costs SOL at prices you can read on the site: the diner's menu runs 0.01 to 0.10 SOL a meal, cryo free, the heater pro rata of 0.10 SOL for 15 minutes, the yard 0.04 a visit, arena entry 0.005 and stakes a slice of the cat's SOL, casino spins 0.01 to 0.05, the shop's wearables 0.05 to 0.32; every one of those times the price multiplier you set in Economy, and every one goes back to the treasury. SOL comes back to the cats three ways: chores (two to five meals' worth each), payroll (every half hour, a cat with no SOL left, meaning it cannot afford a meal, gets back what it spent since the last payroll; a cat with SOL in paw gets nothing; the site's Payouts page shows the next payroll and who is due), and selling $QUBT or $QCATS at the exchange, which a cat does only after chores and the scrapyard. The lab pays $QUBT when a bar fills, the scrapyard pays $QCATS per salvage job, both from the treasury, both the moment the work is done. Creator fees are the only money that enters the loop; swap fees, gas and the build fund are the only money that leaves it.

## Deploy on Railway (about 10 minutes)

1. Put this folder in a GitHub repo.
2. railway.app → **New Project** → **Deploy from GitHub repo** → pick the repo. Railway detects Node and runs `npm start`.
3. **Variables** tab → add:
   - `PORT` = `3000`
   - `ADMIN_PASSWORD` — a long password. This is the only thing between the internet and your admin page.
   - `DATA_DIR` = `/data`
   - `RPC_URL` — from helius.dev (free account → create an API key → copy the mainnet RPC URL)
   - `LLM_API_KEY` — optional, an Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com, for AI-written meetings and thoughts. `LLM_MODEL` picks the model (default `claude-haiku-5-5`, the cheap one; a few dollars a month at this call rate).
4. **Settings → Volumes → Add volume**, mount path `/data`. Without this, config and state reset on every redeploy.
5. **Settings → Networking → Generate domain.** You get `something.up.railway.app`.
6. Open `https://something.up.railway.app/admin` → log in with any username and your password.

## The lab pays a stock

The lab pays **$QUBT** (a tokenized stock on Solana, mint `QUBTAD8C9bMU9LvmMNgKPhrmBGbHvxpu6vfWQtThxxw`), not a memecoin. Paste its mint into `/admin` → Token → "$QUBT contract address". The treasury wallet holds the supply and hands it out per filled bar; it only buys more (through Jupiter) if it runs short. Amounts are fractional: a $5 bar is about 0.3 $QUBT at $15 a share. Payouts show as dollars first ("$5.00 in $QUBT") with the share count after, holdings as shares with their dollar value, one decimal place. Cats only ever sell it (through Jupiter), never buy it. The ticker and the name on the site come from `QI_TICKER` / `QI_NAME` in the brain's config (both default to `$QUBT`); the price is read from the deepest DexScreener pair and, since a stock token trades against USDC, converted to SOL with the SOL price.

## Connect the website

In the site's `index.html`, set `BRAIN_URL: "https://something.up.railway.app"` in the CONFIG block and redeploy the site. The page stops simulating locally and shows the brain's box.

## Wallets

1. In `/admin` → **generate wallets**. You see eleven secret keys ONCE (treasury + ten cats) and their public addresses. The same button (**new wallets**) works later too, to replace all eleven: pause transactions, pull everything back to the treasury, move it to the new treasury address yourself, then replace the two variables and redeploy.
2. Copy `TREASURY_KEY` and `CAT_KEYS` into Railway **Variables**. They are never stored on the server's disk, never shown again, never sent to the website.
3. Redeploy. `/admin` now shows "wallets loaded" and reads balances through your RPC.
4. Send the $QCATS supply to the treasury and hand it out from **Distribute**. Send claimed creator fees to the treasury wallet; the box logs them as "fees landed" within 30 seconds.

**Where swaps go.** Jupiter. If Jupiter is unavailable or has no route, a pump.fun coin can also go through **PumpPortal** (0.5% fee); nothing else is tried. After any swap lands, the brain reads the confirmed transaction's real balance changes and books those (fees included), not the quote.

**Swaps.** A Jupiter swap costs a cat about 0.0045 SOL over the amount it trades: the network fee, a refundable deposit for the temporary wrapped-SOL account, and on a first buy of a coin ~0.002 for the token account. A cat short of that is quietly sent just the shortfall by the treasury first (`/admin` → Economy, on by default; it shows in the on-chain results with its signature, not in the box), then the swap runs. Slippage is Jupiter's dynamic estimate under a 15% ceiling, with one wider retry; a swap that expires is rebuilt from a fresh quote up to three times, with a rising priority fee (capped at 0.0003 SOL); a refused swap puts the cat off the exchange for 20–30 minutes. The Distribute panel also has **Top up**: every ticked cat below a target balance gets the difference, in one transaction.

**Fees from outside.** A rise in the treasury that the cats did not pay in is looked up by its transaction: if it went through pump.fun's programs (a creator-fee claim) it is logged as creator fees, with the signature, so the ledger row links to Solscan; if it is a plain transfer from any other wallet it is shown with its signature and **also counted as creator rewards** (whoever sends SOL to the treasury, it counts). A cat paying the treasury is recognised by its sender and never counted as fees, whatever the timing (the brain also waits until no cat transaction is in flight before judging a rise). Money sent to the cat wallets is never a fee row: the box just shows the new balance.

**How a transaction is sent.** Every transfer carries a priority fee read from the network (a few thousand lamports at most) and a compute budget, is re-broadcast every two seconds until it confirms, and if its blockhash expires anyway ("block height exceeded") it is re-signed with a fresh one and a higher tip, up to three times. Before anything is reported as failed the signature is looked up once more, so a payment that did land is never refunded in the game. A slow or rate-limited public RPC is the usual reason for expiries; a Helius / Triton / QuickNode endpoint in `RPC_URL` fixes most of them.

## Launch day

Paste the contract address into `/admin` → Token → CA, add the X link, Save. The site updates within seconds, market cap goes live, wallet token balances appear, simulated fees stop. From then on: claim fees → send to treasury → **Distribute** (or turn on autopilot).
